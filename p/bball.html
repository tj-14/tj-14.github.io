Basketball Bodyweight Program – 7 Day Visual Guide

Focus: Explosiveness, Agility, Core Stability
Space: Yoga mat (~1.8m x 0.6m)
Equipment: Bodyweight only, optional chair/towel

Day 1 – Explosive Strength + Core

Exercise Description Reps Visual
Jump Squats Explosive jump from squat position, land softly and repeat. 3x10
Bulgarian Split Squats Back foot elevated, lower into lunge, drive through front heel. 3x8 each leg
Single-Leg Glute Bridge Lift hips with one foot flat and the other extended. 3x8 each leg
Dead Bug Lower opposite arm and leg slowly while bracing core. 3x20 (10 each side)
Plank Walk-Outs Walk hands out to plank from standing, then return. 3x6

Day 2 – Foot Speed + Core Stability

Exercise Description Reps Visual
Line Hops Quick hops over an imaginary line, forward-back and side-to-side. 3x20s each
Skater Stepbacks Lateral jumps from one foot to the other, mimicking skating stride. 3x10 each side
Plank Shoulder Taps Tap opposite shoulder while maintaining tight plank. 3x20
Side Plank Support body on one elbow and foot, forming a straight line. 2x30s each side
Wall Sit Hold squat against a wall with thighs parallel to floor. 2x45s

Day 3 – Light Core + Mobility (Recovery)

Exercise Description Reps Visual
Bird Dog Extend opposite arm and leg while keeping core stable. 3x10 each side
Glute Bridge Hold Hold hips in top bridge position, squeeze glutes. 3x30s
Supine Hip Raises Lie on back, feet flat, raise hips and lower slowly. 3x10
Side-Lying Leg Lifts Raise and lower top leg while lying on side. 2x12 each side
Seated Stretch Forward folds and hip openers to increase mobility. 30s each

Day 4 – Hamstrings & Control

Exercise Description Reps Visual
Nordic Curl Kneel and slowly lower forward using hamstrings to resist. 3x3–5
Single-Leg Glute Bridge Raise hips using one leg while other leg is extended. 3x10 each
Wall Sit with Calf Raises In wall sit, lift heels repeatedly. 3x15
Bird Dog Holds Hold extended bird dog position on each side. 3x10s/side
Russian Twists Twist torso side to side while seated, feet lifted. 3x20

Day 5 – Balance + Reactive Core

Exercise Description Reps Visual
Squat Hold to Jump Hold squat 3–5 sec, then jump explosively. 3x6
Skater Steps on Mat Jump laterally from one foot to the other. 3x12
Wall Sit Reach In wall sit, reach hand to opposite knee. 3x12
Plank Reach Forward From plank, reach forward alternately. 3x20 (10 each)
Supine Dead Bug Hold Hold the bottom position of the dead bug. 3x15s

Day 6 – Skill / Ball Handling (Optional)

Exercise Description Reps Visual
Stationary Dribble Drills Practice crossovers, hesitations, and between-the-legs dribbling in place. 3x30s each move
Footwork Simulation Mimic jab steps, drives, and defensive slides within a small space. 3x1 min circuits
Mental Reps Visualize plays, decisions, and on-court movements. 3x5 mins focused visualization

Day 7 – Full Rest / Stretch

Stretch Description Reps/Time Visual
Couch Stretch Hip flexor stretch with back foot elevated. 2x30s each side
Figure-4 Glute Stretch Stretch glutes by crossing ankle over opposite knee. 2x30s each side
Pigeon Pose Deep hip opener in front leg bent posture. 2x30s each side
Hamstring Forward Fold Reach toward toes while keeping knees soft. 2x30s
Breathwork Relaxed belly breathing for recovery. 5 min
