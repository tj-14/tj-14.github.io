Basketball Improvement Plan for #74 (Thunder Braves - Updated)

Focus on controlled aggression, reading the defense, making decisive actions, smart rebounding, and dynamic movement.

Focus Area Specific Skill / Drill Objective Execution Tip / Mental Notes Priority
Offensive Assertiveness / Decision Making (Catching the Ball) Attack the Closeout / Triple Threat Read & React Combat hesitation; become a score/drive threat upon catch; exploit defensive reactions. Catch low & ready (triple threat). READ defender FIRST (fraction of a second). Shoot if open, Drive hard on aggressive closeouts/gaps. Be decisive.
Mental Note: *"Threat first (shoot/drive), then react."* or *"Read, then decide - quick!"*		 High
Shooting (Current 20% 3PT) Selective Catch-and-Shoot (3-pointers) / Mid-Range Development Improve efficiency; keep defense honest without forcing low-percentage shots. In games: Shoot 3s ONLY when WIDE open & balanced/in-rhythm, or late shot clock. Prioritize attacking closeouts. Develop 1-2 dribble pull-up/floater. In practice: High volume shooting reps (form, game speed C&S).
Mental Note: *"Good shot? Or better drive/pass?"* 		Medium (Game)
High (Practice)
Playmaking Drive and Kick / Reading Help Defense Use your drive (from attacking closeouts) to create open shots for teammates when help defense commits. Attack gaps aggressively. Eyes up to see the help rotate. Make crisp, timely passes to the open player.
Mental Note: *"Drive to score OR create for others."* 		(Connected to Assertiveness)
Off-Ball Offense (Point Guard Role) Dynamic Safety / Perimeter Movement & Relocation Maintain offensive spacing & flow while providing defensive transition safety. Avoid standing still at the top. Stay active above the arc (shift w/ ball, reverse pivot). Pass & Relocate: Move to a new open spot after passing. Set occasional high screens. Be ready for swing passes.
Mental Note: *"Active safety, not a statue."* or *"Move with purpose, stay high."*		 Medium
Defensive Rebounding (Guard Specific) Locating Your Man & Reading the Crash Ensure team rebound first; prevent easy offensive boards by *your* man, then potentially initiate break. Shot goes up -> Find YOUR man immediately. Man crashes hard? -> Check/box out briefly to impede, *then* release for outlet. Man stays back? -> Release immediately to outlet spot (sideline).
Mental Note: *"My man first, then ball/outlet."* or *"Secure the board, THEN break."*		 Medium (Crucial Guard Role)
Defense On-Ball & Off-Ball Awareness Improve reaction time, prevent drives, better help defense. Keep head on a swivel (see ball AND man). Anticipate cuts/drives. Stay low in stance. Communicate rotations early.
Mental Note: *"See ball, see man. Stay low. Talk!"*		 Medium
