Rock Climbing Flexibility Program

A weekly schedule to improve mobility and flexibility for climbing. Tap the ▶ video links or check thumbnails for a quick demo.

Day Focus Area Duration
Mon Full Body Mobility 20–25 min
Tue Lower Body 20 min
Wed Rest / Light Yoga Optional
Thu Hips & Core 20 min
Fri Shoulders & Spine 15–20 min
Sat Full Body & Splits 25–30 min
Sun Rest / Gentle Stretch Optional

Daily Details & Exercise Cues

Monday · Full Body Mobility
  • Jumping Jacks – 1 min ▶ video Jump feet wide while swinging arms overhead, then return. Keep it light.
  • Arm Circles – 30 s each way ▶ video Draw slow, controlled circles forward and backward to warm up shoulder joints.
  • Cat‑Cow Flow – 1 min ▶ video On all fours, inhale to arch spine (Cow), exhale to round spine (Cat). Move with breath.
  • World’s Greatest Stretch – 1 min each side ▶ video From lunge, place one hand down, rotate torso & reach other arm skyward. Sink hips.
  • Deep Squat Hold – 1 min ▶ video Feet shoulder-width, sit low, keep heels flat, chest up. Rock gently for ankle/hip mobility.
  • Shoulder Dislocates (Band/Stick) – 10 reps ▶ video Wide grip on band/stick, sweep arms overhead & behind back, keeping elbows straight. Opens chest.
  • Standing Hip Circles – 10 reps each way ▶ video Hands on hips, feet stable, draw large, slow circles with your pelvis.

Goal: prime every major joint for the week ahead.

Tuesday · Lower Body Focus
  • Dynamic Leg Swings – 10 each leg/direction ▶ video Hold onto support. Swing leg forward/back & side-to-side (across body). Controlled motion.
  • Deep Squat Hold – 2 sets × 1 min ▶ video Focus on maintaining depth, keeping chest up and heels down. Improves hip/ankle mobility.
  • Lizard Lunge – 1 min each side ▶ video From lunge, step foot outside hand, sink hips forward and down to stretch hip flexors. Option: forearms down.
  • Frog Pose – 2 min ▶ video Knees wide (inner knees/ankles on floor), forearms down, push hips back gently. Stretches inner thighs.
  • 90/90 Hip Switches – 10 reps total ▶ video Seated, legs bent 90° (one internal, one external rotation). Rotate knees side-to-side smoothly.
  • Half Splits (Ardha Hanumanasana) – 1 min each side ▶ video From low lunge, shift hips back over rear knee, straighten front leg, flex foot. Hinge forward.
  • Pigeon Pose (Eka Pada Rajakapotasana) – 1–2 min each side ▶ video Bring front shin across mat (adjust angle for comfort), square hips, extend back leg. Fold forward if comfortable.

Goal: unlock hips for high steps, stemming, and drop-knees.

Wednesday · Rest / Light Yoga
  • Sun Salutations (Surya Namaskar A) – 5–10 rounds ▶ video Gentle flow linking breath with movement (Mountain, Forward Fold, Half Lift, Plank, Chaturanga, Up-Dog, Down-Dog).
  • Seated Forward Fold (Paschimottanasana) – 2 min ▶ video Legs extended, hinge from hips (not waist), reach toward feet. Keep spine long. Use strap if needed.
  • Child’s Pose (Balasana) – 1-2 min ▶ video Knees together or wide, sit back on heels, fold torso down, rest forehead. Arms forward or back.
  • Happy Baby Pose (Ananda Balasana) – 1 min ▶ video Lie on back, bend knees toward armpits, grab outer feet or ankles. Gently rock side-to-side.

Goal: active recovery, gentle mobility, and mental reset.

Thursday · Hips & Core
  • Butterfly Stretch (Baddha Konasana) – 2 min ▶ video Sit tall, soles of feet together, knees out. Gently press knees down or hinge forward.
  • Bird Dog – 10 reps each side ▶ video On all fours, extend opposite arm and leg simultaneously, keeping core tight and back flat. Hold briefly.
  • Plank Hold – 3 sets × 30-60 sec ▶ video Forearms or hands under shoulders, body in straight line from head to heels. Engage core, don't let hips sag.
  • Cossack Squat / Side Lunge Stretch – 1 min each side ▶ video Wide stance, shift weight to one side, bending that knee and keeping other leg straight. Sink low.
  • Dead Bug – 10-12 reps total ▶ video Lie on back, knees bent 90°, arms up. Lower opposite arm and leg slowly, keeping lower back pressed down.

Goal: strengthen core for stability and improve hip range for dynamic moves.

Friday · Shoulders & Spine
  • Wall Angels / Wall Slides – 10-15 reps ▶ video Stand with back against wall, arms bent 90° ('cactus arms'). Slide arms up wall, keeping contact.
  • Doorway Chest Stretch – 30 sec each side ▶ video Place forearm on doorframe, elbow shoulder height. Step forward gently to feel stretch across chest/shoulder.
  • Thread the Needle Pose – 1 min each side ▶ video From all fours, reach one arm up, then 'thread' it under opposite armpit, lowering shoulder/ear to mat.
  • Puppy Pose (Uttana Shishosana) – 1-2 min ▶ video From all fours, walk hands forward, keeping hips over knees. Melt chest/forehead towards floor. Stretches lats/shoulders.
  • Supine Spinal Twist – 1 min each side ▶ video Lie on back, draw one knee to chest, then guide it across body. Keep shoulders grounded, gaze opposite direction.

Goal: improve shoulder mobility for reaching and spinal flexibility for twisting moves.

Saturday · Full Body & Splits Prep
  • Cat‑Cow Flow – 1 min ▶ video Warm up the spine before deeper stretches.
  • World’s Greatest Stretch – 1 min each side ▶ video Full body dynamic movement to prepare.
  • Low Lunge Quad Stretch – 1 min each side ▶ video In low lunge (back knee down), reach back for foot, gently pull heel towards glute. Stretches hip flexor/quad.
  • Half Splits (Deeper Hold) – 1-2 min each side ▶ video Focus on hinging from hips, keeping front leg straight. Use blocks under hands if needed.
  • Standing Wide-Legged Forward Fold (Prasarita Padottanasana) – 1-2 min ▶ video Stand with feet wide, parallel. Hinge at hips, fold forward. Hands to floor/blocks/ankles. Stretches hamstrings/inner thighs.
  • Front Splits Prep / Attempt – 1-2 min each side ▶ video From half splits, slide front foot forward, back knee back. Use blocks under hands for support. Only go as far as comfortable.
  • Middle Splits Prep / Frog / Straddle – 2 min ▶ video Choose Frog pose or seated/standing straddle fold. Focus on inner thigh flexibility.

Goal: work towards greater flexibility in splits-related ranges, beneficial for wide stems and high feet.

Sunday · Rest / Gentle Stretch
  • Legs Up The Wall Pose (Viparita Karani) – 5 min ▶ video Sit sideways near wall, swing legs up wall, lie back. Hips close to wall. Restorative.
  • Reclined Butterfly (Supta Baddha Konasana) – 2-3 min ▶ video Lie on back, soles of feet together, knees fall open. Support knees with blocks if needed. Gentle hip opener.
  • Supine Spinal Twist (Gentle) – 1 min each side ▶ video Repeat Friday's twist, focusing on relaxation rather than depth.
  • Corpse Pose (Savasana) – 5 min ▶ video Lie flat on back, arms and legs relaxed slightly away from body. Focus on breath and stillness.

Goal: complete rest or very light, restorative stretching to aid recovery.

Disclaimer: Consult a healthcare professional before starting any new exercise program. Listen to your body and modify as needed.
