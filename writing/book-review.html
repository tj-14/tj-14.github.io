Tossaporn Saengja back

Book Review

Non-fiction

  • When Breath Becomes Air: Read when I was working in South Korea. It's emotional, insightful, and thought-provoking. Near-death experience is difficult to encounter. It's a good read on making life choices, going through different experience, and appreciating them.

Fiction

  • Tomorrow, and Tomorrow, and Tomorrow: The settings around MIT are nostalgic to me. A lot of relationships and emotions are involved. Probably the fastest fiction I finished. It's a fun read.
  • เทพยุทธ์เซียน Glory: My first Chinese long novel. It's like a shonen manga. The main character is very skilled, but also very practical in his judgement throughout the story. It's a very fun read about a character who's very well-calculated in an online competitive game setting.
